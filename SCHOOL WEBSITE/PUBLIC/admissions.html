Secondary School Admissions

"...Passion for Excellence"

We welcome applications from motivated students ready to thrive in a disciplined, excellence-driven environment. Read through our admissions process below and visit our office to get started.

How to Apply

01

Visit Our Office

Come to the school's administrative office to purchase and collect the official admission form.

02

Fill the Form

Complete the admission form clearly in capital letters and gather all required documents.

03

Submit Application

Return the completed form and documents to the school's administrative office.

04

Entrance Test

Eligible applicants will be invited to sit an entrance test in Mathematics, English and General Paper.

Required Documents

  • ✅ Birth Certificate
  • ✅ Transfer Certificate from previous school
  • ✅ Medical Fitness Certificate
  • ✅ Two coloured passport photographs of student
  • ✅ Academic performance report from former school
🚌

School Bus Available

Vista Hills International Schools offers a school bus service. Indicate your interest on the admission form and our team will provide details on routes and fees.

Get Your Admission Form

Admission forms are available for purchase at the school's administrative office. Please visit us during school hours to obtain your form and begin the application process.
